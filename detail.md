## 6. Visual Identity

### Overall art direction

**Modern Dark Premium**

The design should feel like a refined digital studio: bold typography,
carefully controlled orange highlights, deep dark surfaces, and
purposeful motion.

Avoid: - Excessive neon glow. - Too many gradients. - Overloaded glass
cards. - Random floating shapes. - Generic AI illustrations. - Fake
client logos or fake performance metrics. - Too many competing accent
colors.

### Color palette

  Purpose                Color           Hex
  ---------------------- --------------- -----------
  Main background        Near Black      `#0B0B0D`
  Secondary background   Charcoal        `#121216`
  Card background        Dark Graphite   `#19191F`
  Elevated surface       Soft Graphite   `#222229`
  Primary accent         Vivid Orange    `#FF5722`
  Accent hover           Warm Orange     `#FF7043`
  Main text              Off White       `#F5F5F2`
  Secondary text         Cool Gray       `#A5A5AE`
  Muted text             Slate Gray      `#73737D`
  Borders                Dark Border     `#303038`

**Usage guidance:**

-   Use `#0B0B0D` for the overall page background.
-   Use `#121216` and `#19191F` for alternating sections and cards.
-   Use `#FF5722` for primary buttons, small labels, active states, and
    selected words.
-   Use off-white for headings and important body copy.
-   Use gray for supporting text.
-   Keep orange accents selective so they remain visually meaningful.

### Typography

Recommended font pairing:

**Primary display and heading font: Space Grotesk**

-   Strong geometric character.
-   Modern technical feel.
-   Suitable for large hero headlines and section titles.

**Body font: Inter**

-   Highly readable.
-   Clean and neutral.
-   Suitable for paragraphs, navigation, labels, and cards.

**Optional technical accent: JetBrains Mono**

-   Use sparingly for small labels, code-inspired details, project
    metadata, and section identifiers.
-   Do not use it for long paragraphs.

Suggested hierarchy:

  Element              Font                          Suggested size
  -------------------- ------------------------ -------------------
  Hero headline        Space Grotesk Bold         64--88 px desktop
  Section heading      Space Grotesk Semibold             40--56 px
  Project title        Space Grotesk Semibold             22--28 px
  Body copy            Inter Regular                      16--18 px
  Navigation           Inter Medium                       13--15 px
  Eyebrow / metadata   JetBrains Mono                     10--12 px

Use responsive typography so large headings scale down gracefully on
tablet and mobile. These are starting values, not rigid requirements.

### Spacing and layout

-   Use a centered content container, approximately 1160--1240 px
    maximum width on large desktop screens.
-   Use consistent horizontal page padding.
-   Give major sections generous vertical spacing.
-   Use a 12-column grid for desktop layouts where useful.
-   Keep paragraphs at a comfortable reading width.
-   Use consistent card padding and border radius.
-   Align section headings and content to a clear grid.

Suggested spacing scale:

-   4 px --- tiny detail spacing.
-   8 px --- compact spacing.
-   12 px --- small spacing.
-   16 px --- standard spacing.
-   24 px --- card and content spacing.
-   32 px --- component spacing.
-   48 px --- section sub-spacing.
-   72--112 px --- major section spacing.

### Corners, borders, and shadows

-   Buttons: 8--12 px radius.
-   Cards: 14--20 px radius.
-   Large feature panels: 20--28 px radius.
-   Use subtle 1 px borders in `#303038`.
-   Shadows should be soft and restrained.
-   Orange glow may be used behind one or two important hero elements,
    not every card.

## 9. Motion and Interaction

Use motion sparingly to make the site feel polished.

Recommended: - Smooth anchor scrolling. - Subtle fade-up reveal for
sections. - Small button hover transitions. - Project card lift of a few
pixels on hover. - Gentle orange highlight transitions. - A restrained
workflow-node animation in the hero.

Implementation guidance: - Prefer CSS transitions and keyframes for
simple effects. - Use JavaScript only where interaction requires it. -
Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. - Avoid scroll hijacking, excessive
parallax, and animations that delay access to content. - Ensure the
website remains usable when animations are disabled.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10. Responsive Design

The website must work on desktop, tablet, and mobile.

### Desktop

-   Full navigation.
-   Two-column hero layout.
-   Large headline.
-   Two-column or three-column project grid.
-   Spacious section layouts.

### Tablet

-   Reduce hero headline size.
-   Adjust columns and card widths.
-   Keep navigation comfortable.
-   Avoid cramped side-by-side content.

### Mobile

-   Single-column layout.
-   Compact header and accessible menu.
-   Hero text first, visual second.
-   Full-width or comfortably sized CTA buttons.
-   Project cards stacked vertically.
-   No horizontal overflow.
-   Comfortable tap targets.
-   Readable body text and line lengths.

Test at common viewport widths, including approximately 375 px, 768 px,
1024 px, and 1440 px.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 11. Accessibility and Quality Standards

-   Use semantic HTML landmarks.
-   Maintain readable text contrast.
-   Provide meaningful alt text for informative images.
-   Ensure keyboard navigation and visible focus states.
-   Use descriptive link text.
-   Label all form fields.
-   Show clear form validation and success/error messages.
-   Do not communicate information through color alone.
-   Test at mobile sizes and with reduced motion enabled.
-   Optimize images and avoid unnecessary scripts.
-   Check links, buttons, and forms before publishing.
